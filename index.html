Activity 2: The Digital Nomad Story

  1. Read the extended text about Maya.
  2. Read the answers on the right. Type the correct WH- word in the blank to make the question match the answer!
💻

"Meet Maya. She is 28 years old, and she is a software developer from Tokyo, Japan. Right now, she is not in Japan because she is working in New York for a big technology project. Her birthday is in December, so she loves the cold winter weather. Maya is very busy, but she is happy because her job is interesting and her new colleagues are friendly."

1. is the software developer?
➔ Maya.
2. old is Maya?
➔ She is 28 years old.
3. is Maya from?
➔ She is from Tokyo, Japan.
4. is her job?
➔ She is a software developer.
5. is her birthday?
➔ It is in December.
6. is she working right now?
➔ She is working in New York.
7. is her project?
➔ It is a big technology project.
8. weather does she love?
➔ She loves cold winter weather.
9. are her new colleagues?
➔ They are friendly.
10. is she happy?
➔ Because her job is interesting.
